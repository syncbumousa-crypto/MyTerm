MyTerm is a small, free tool for keeping track of one university term. Using it means agreeing to what is written here.

What it is

A board where you name a term, add your courses, tick off chapters and record your marks. Everything you enter is written to your own Google Drive.

Your account

  • You are responsible for what you put in. Do not use the service to store anything unlawful.
  • You may stop at any time: unlink Google, or write to us to have your account removed.

Your work is yours

Your term lives in files in your Drive. We claim nothing in them, and unlinking or deleting your account never touches them. Keep your own copies of anything you would be sorry to lose — a Drive file can be moved, renamed or emptied by anybody with access to your Drive, including you.

What we do not promise

  • The service is given as it is, with no warranty. It may be unavailable, and it may change.
  • It depends on Google Drive and Supabase; when they are down, so is this.
  • It is not a system of record for your grades. Your university's is.
