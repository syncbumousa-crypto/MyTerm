Privacy — MyTerm

Last updated 10 October 2026

MyTerm is a study board for one university term. Your term — its courses, its chapters and its marks — is stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder you pick. It is never copied to us.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in. Your email address and a scrambled form of your password, held by Supabase so you can log in.
  • A pointer. The name of your Drive folder and the id of your term file — so that opening MyTerm on another device finds your work instead of asking you where it is.
  • A key to keep the link alive. When you link your Google account, Google gives us a key that lets us ask for a short-lived permit on your behalf. The key is kept on our server, never in your browser, and never shown to anyone.
  • A counter. A number that goes up each time you save, so your other device knows there is something new to read. It carries no content.

What we do not keep

  • The contents of your term. Those are files in your Drive.
  • Anything from the rest of your Drive. We ask Google only for drive.file: an app with that permission can see the files it created itself and the folder you handed it, and nothing else.
  • Trackers, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the page.

Who else sees it

Two services hold what is listed above: Supabase (sign-in, pointer, key) and Google (your Drive). Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Taking it back

  • Unlink Google from inside MyTerm: the stored key is deleted at once, and we can no longer reach your Drive.
  • Or revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your files stay yours either way — unlinking never deletes anything in your Drive.
